# WORLD SIGNALS — Nepal Rasuwa flood Live Intelligence AW research v0.1

## Research date

2026-09-06

## Selected factual object

A major flood in the Bhote Koshi River system affected Rasuwa and neighbouring districts in Nepal on 26 August 2026.

The first Live Intelligence specimen is the physical shock itself, not a downstream humanitarian appeal, not an Analysis conclusion, and not a fabricated Canonical event.

## Primary source 1 — Government of Nepal, Ministry of Home Affairs

Title: **Official Disaster Relief Appeal**

URL: `https://www.moha.gov.np/en/post/ha-ra-tha-ka-apa-l-11`

The Ministry records:

- Wednesday, 10 Bhadra 2083 / 26 August 2026;
- event time: 8:40 AM;
- a massive flood in the Bhote Koshi River of Rasuwa District;
- heavy loss of life and property;
- severe impact to riverbank areas in Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading and Gorkha districts;
- immediate government search, rescue and emergency relief operations;
- total human and material damage still under assessment.

The page is dated 11 Bhadra 2083, corresponding to 27 August 2026. WORLD SIGNALS therefore records publication precision as a civil date and does not invent a publication clock time.

### Time normalization

Authoritative local time:

`2026-08-26T08:40:00` in `Asia/Kathmandu`

Nepal Time is UTC+05:45 on that date, giving:

`2026-08-26T02:55:00Z`

This is real-world event time. It is separate from the later WORLD SIGNALS `observed_at_utc` timestamp.

## Primary source 2 — World Health Organization, Nepal

Title: **WHO releases US$150 000 emergency funds to support Nepal’s flood response**

URL: `https://www.who.int/nepal/news/detail/30-08-2026-who-releases-us150-000-emergency-funds-to-support-nepal-s-flood-response`

Publication date: 30 August 2026.

WHO records that:

- flash floods struck on 26 August 2026;
- emergency medical supplies were mobilized within hours;
- supplies included an Interagency Emergency Health Kit intended to support nearly 10,000 people for three months;
- WHO released US$150,000 through the South-East Asia Regional Health Emergency Fund for response needs.

This evidence is used as source confirmation and context for health-response consequences. It does not replace Nepal's government source for the exact flood timestamp.

## Current WHO emergency page — causal restraint check

URL: `https://www.who.int/nepal/emergencies/2026-rasuwa-flash-floods`

WHO's emergency page says preliminary information indicates the Rasuwa flooding followed a sudden surge into the Bhote Koshi from the Tibet Autonomous Region of China and may have been linked to an upstream ice avalanche and temporary damming of the Lhende River. WHO explicitly says assessments are ongoing to confirm the full extent and mechanism.

AW therefore does **not** encode that possible mechanism as an established cause in the observation. The source is useful as a guardrail demonstration: Live Intelligence stores verified facts and source-qualified uncertainty, while causal interpretation belongs downstream.

## Downstream context deliberately not populated

United Nations in Nepal, 4 September 2026:

`https://nepal.un.org/en/322156-nepal-floods-un-and-partners-launch-496-million-emergency-appeal`

The UN and humanitarian partners launched a US$49.6 million four-month flash appeal intended to reach more than 84,000 people.

This is a distinct later `INSTITUTIONAL_DEVELOPMENT`, not part of the physical-shock identity. AW records it only in research. It is not added as a second Live observation and no story identity is created.

## Why no Canonical link

The flood is an unscheduled physical shock. The Canonical Registry is for scheduled, provisional, recurring, conditional and foreseeable temporal objects and should not be expanded merely to provide an anchor for a Live observation.

`canonical_links = []` is therefore the correct relationship state.

## Why `PRIMARY_CONFIRMED`

The event itself, local timestamp, affected districts and government response are directly stated by the Government of Nepal. WHO independently confirms the flood and emergency health response. There is no need to downgrade the existence of the physical shock merely because its upstream physical trigger remains under investigation.

Verification attaches to the observation as actually written, not to every speculative proposition circulating around the event.

## Rights / projection discipline

AW stores factual metadata, source titles and URLs only. It does not republish source bodies, images or market data. Public observation projection remains closed in v0.2.

## Research conclusion

The Nepal specimen can be admitted without weakening the architecture if and only if:

- native `Asia/Kathmandu` time and UTC agree;
- no canonical ID is fabricated;
- source-publication dates remain civil-date precision;
- the uncertain upstream trigger is not promoted into fact;
- population remains bounded to one observation / two evidence rows;
- public feed claims remain false;
- all Canonical, Monitor and Analysis datasets remain byte-identical.
