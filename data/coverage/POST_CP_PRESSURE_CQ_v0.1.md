## Why CQ is research rather than Live population

The 9 September signing is factually strong enough that a bounded Live observation might eventually be justified. CQ deliberately does not add it yet because the same source reveals a larger structured family.

The architecture-first choice prevents:

- isolated story identity followed by later duplicate Canonical process identity;
- signing/effectiveness conflation;
- treating `substantive conclusion of negotiations` as a signed or effective early-harvest arrangement;
- unreviewed taxonomy expansion;
- assigning a new MOFCOM source ID before checking the Source Registry properly;
- turning public accessibility into Monitor permission;
- bulk backfilling 39 prior partners before the identity and lifecycle contract is stable.

This is exactly the kind of case where `research → architecture → source registry → sample population` should precede expansion.
