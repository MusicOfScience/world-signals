## Reviewed interpretation

1. **South Asia is the strongest structural geographic warning.** Its 18 occurrences reduce to only four series and two institutions; 17 of 18 occurrences are macro releases from one institution.
2. **Africa and Southeast Asia are thin but not simply empty.** Their lower occurrence counts conceal greater institutional diversity than South Asia, so the correction question is depth/systemic relevance rather than count equalisation.
3. **Monetary and macro dominance is partly a recurrence effect.** Their 65.3% occurrence share must not be read as 65.3% of distinct signal families.
4. **Physical climate risk is genuinely shallow at the series/institution level.** It requires a separate ontology/source review because forecast and hazard products do not map automatically to scheduled canonical events.
5. **Health/biosecurity is institutionally concentrated**, and **energy/commodities is frequency-heavy relative to institutional diversity**. Both merit qualitative review rather than quota filling.
