## Research question

Does the 9 September 2026 China–Cabo Verde Common Development Economic Partnership Framework Agreement represent a one-off bilateral development suitable only for a bounded Live observation, or is it evidence of a sufficiently stable China–Africa treaty/process family that WORLD SIGNALS should model explicitly before adding population?

## Trigger development — Cabo Verde, 9 September 2026

The Ministry of Commerce of the People's Republic of China (MOFCOM) states that on 9 September 2026 China International Trade Representative and Vice Minister Li Chenggang and Cabo Verde Minister of Economy, Trade, Industry and Digital Transformation António Baptista signed the `Framework Agreement between the Government of the People's Republic of China and the Government of the Republic of Cabo Verde on a Common Development Economic Partnership` in Beijing.

MOFCOM says the framework is intended to deepen economic and trade cooperation, strengthen economic resilience and development momentum, focus on sustainable and inclusive growth, and support Cabo Verde's agricultural transformation, blue economy and manufacturing development. It calls Cabo Verde the **40th African country** with which China has signed this framework and separately states that the two sides confirmed the **substantive conclusion of negotiations on an early-harvest arrangement** under the partnership.

Primary source:

- MOFCOM News Office, 10 September 2026: `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2026/art_4b477c9439de4b17a79d39a63456a7d0.html`

Important semantic boundary: `framework agreement signed` and `early-harvest negotiations substantively concluded` are two distinct states. The Cabo Verde release does **not** itself say that an early-harvest arrangement was signed or had entered into force. CQ must not collapse those states.

## Independent counterpart context

A fresh search of Cabo Verde government material did not yet surface a 9 September counterpart publication for the framework agreement itself. That absence is a retrieval finding, not evidence that Cabo Verde did not sign.

Relevant Cabo Verde first-party material does independently establish a broader active China–Cabo Verde economic-cooperation relationship, including:

- 2 February 2026 economic/technical cooperation instruments: `https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-e-china-reforcam-cooperacao-economica-e-tecnica-com-novos-instrumentos/`;
- 11 August 2026 strategic infrastructure/technology memorandum with CMEC: `https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-e-china-reforcam-cooperacao-em-tecnologia-de-ponta-e-infraestruturas/`.

Chinese diplomatic material before the signing also records both governments discussing conclusion of a Common Development Economic Partnership Agreement and implementation of China's zero-tariff policy for African diplomatic partners:

- PRC Ministry of Foreign Affairs / Embassy in Cabo Verde, meeting of 26 August 2026: `https://www.mfa.gov.cn/zwbd_673032/wshd_673034/202608/t20260830_12012581.shtml`.

These are context/supporting sources, not substitutes for the MOFCOM instrument source.

## Evidence that this is a programme, not an isolated agreement

MOFCOM's own publication history establishes a growing family of similarly named bilateral frameworks:

- September 2024: China reported signed frameworks with **22 African countries** during/around the Beijing FOCAC summit: `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2024/art_f9fab758fe7c4ce2abb02551e4cb35c6.html`;
- July 2025: MOFCOM reported seven further partner signings and a total of **31**: `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/bnjg/art/2025/art_57b02c070aa6462e9c7e1302149635d0.html`;
- March 2026: Nigeria was described as the **34th** African partner: `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfb/bldhd/art/2026/art_02eafb838ad8446780dad7a1e7971c45.html`;
- April 2026: an official MOFCOM response states that **37 African countries** had signed by 1 May 2026: `https://gzly.mofcom.gov.cn/info/detail?id=ff8080819d048cff019dc58028f72439&replayId=ff8080819d048cff019dc5802920243a`;
- July 2026: Namibia was described as the **38th** African diplomatic partner to sign: `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2026/art_eab1dccd2174421394575fffe53daa82.html`;
- September 2026: Cabo Verde is described as the **40th**.

CQ does **not** infer or manufacture partner #39 from the sequence. The surfaced evidence has not independently identified that member during this research pass. Likewise, the April public Q&A contained a user-supplied list with a duplicate Congo entry; CQ treats MOFCOM's official total as competent for the total but not the user's list as an authoritative completeness table.

MOFCOM also maintains an official agreement-text hub for Common Development Economic Partnership framework agreements with African governments:

- `https://sms.mofcom.gov.cn/yadlyfzoydqzmxdhz/art/2024/art_f9e2731f62dd4e42a47eb223116b5b46.html`

The hub provides bilateral agreement texts in Chinese and, depending on the partner, English/French/Spanish/Arabic. This is strong evidence of an identifiable instrument family and a potentially useful curated source surface. It is not, by itself, authority for unattended monitoring.

## Programme origin and policy context

MOFCOM's publication of President Xi Jinping's 2024 FOCAC Beijing Summit speech identifies the framework-agreement programme as part of the `Trade Prosperity Partnership Action`, alongside expanded Chinese market access for African least-developed-country products:

- `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2024/art_a943517d15694e48a795e537ae6bbb62.html`.

MOFCOM's 12 September 2024 briefing characterized the framework as a new flexible/pragmatic structure for trade and investment liberalisation/facilitation with African partners:

- `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfbzt/2024/swbzklxxwfbh2024n9y12r/index.html`.

The FOCAC Beijing Declaration supplies wider institutional context but should not be treated as the legal instrument itself:

- PRC Ministry of Foreign Affairs, 5 September 2024: `https://www.mfa.gov.cn/eng/xw/zyxw/202409/t20240905_11485993.html`.

## Representative framework structure

A published MOFCOM-hosted framework text for the Central African Republic shows that at least one member of this family is structured around subsequent negotiation rather than a conventional single-step FTA. It identifies four negotiable modules:

1. more convenient trade;
2. more inclusive growth;
3. more resilient supply chains;
4. more modern development fields, including digital trade/e-commerce/payment/logistics/document digitisation/cloud services.

It also provides for early-harvest approaches after module negotiations begin, contact points, institutional mechanisms, amendment/termination and future negotiations. The representative text states that that bilateral framework enters into force on signature.

Representative official text:

- `https://sms.mofcom.gov.cn/cms_files/filemanager/676898164/attach/202411/c06481801ec34e31b3d181b7b886c0ec.pdf?fileName=%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87.pdf`

**Do not generalise partner-specific legal effect without the partner's own text.** The Central African Republic text demonstrates family grammar, not that every Cabo Verde clause, effective-state rule or implementation detail is identical. For Cabo Verde, the 9 September MOFCOM news release establishes signing and substantive conclusion of early-harvest negotiations; exact legal text/effectiveness of that framework remains to be retrieved before any legal-state Canonical claim.

## Early-harvest lifecycle is independently real

The Congo (Brazzaville) pathway demonstrates that the family can progress through distinct later legal states:

- framework agreement signed 6 September 2024;
- joint statement launching Common Development Economic Partnership negotiations, June 2025;
- early-harvest arrangement signed 4 November 2025;
- domestic procedures completed and early-harvest arrangement effective 1 April 2026;
- continuing negotiations envisaged for broader modules.

Primary MOFCOM/FTA surfaces:

- agreement portal: `https://fta.mofcom.gov.cn/channel/congo_special.shtml`;
- early-harvest entry into force: `https://cacs.mofcom.gov.cn/article/gnwjmdt/sb/sbqt/202604/187666.html`;
- official early-harvest arrangement text: `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2026/art_6decb0e4f63d4a9489a7b02d18554338.html`.

Kenya supplies a second independent early-harvest signing case. MOFCOM states that on 24 March 2026 Kenya became the second African country, after Congo (Brazzaville), to sign such an early-harvest arrangement:

- `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2026/art_d5fdc80255d94359886d448535a5754f.html`.

This is sufficient to reject a one-state ontology such as `agreement happened`. A future model needs explicit phase/state semantics.

## Candidate process ontology — research only

CQ proposes the following **research vocabulary**, not schema changes or approved Canonical event types:

- `FRAMEWORK_NEGOTIATION_OR_INTENT` — political/ministerial intention to negotiate or sign;
- `FRAMEWORK_SIGNED` — the bilateral framework instrument is signed;
- `FRAMEWORK_EFFECTIVE` — only when competent partner-specific legal text/evidence establishes effectiveness and it is not identical to signing by rule;
- `MODULE_NEGOTIATION_LAUNCHED` — negotiations under the framework formally begin;
- `EARLY_HARVEST_NEGOTIATIONS_SUBSTANTIVELY_CONCLUDED` — negotiation status, not signature;
- `EARLY_HARVEST_SIGNED` — early-harvest instrument signed;
- `EARLY_HARVEST_EFFECTIVE` — legal/operational entry into force after any required procedures;
- `MODULE_AGREEMENT_SIGNED` / `MODULE_AGREEMENT_EFFECTIVE` — later module instruments, if/when evidenced;
- `AMENDED` / `TERMINATED` — agreement-state events when specifically evidenced.

The same real-world day may establish more than one state only when sources explicitly do so. For example, Cabo Verde on 9 September supports `FRAMEWORK_SIGNED` plus `EARLY_HARVEST_NEGOTIATIONS_SUBSTANTIVELY_CONCLUDED`; it does not support `EARLY_HARVEST_SIGNED` or `EARLY_HARVEST_EFFECTIVE`.

## Taxonomy pressure

The current repository contains `TRADE_SANCTIONS_INDUSTRIAL_POLICY / SANCTIONS_PROCESS` for an EU sanctions legal process. That grammar is not a good semantic fit for CDEP merely because both concern trade policy.

The CDEP programme spans:

- trade liberalisation/facilitation;
- investment;
- supply chains/industrialisation;
- agriculture;
- green/inclusive development;
- digital trade;
- development diplomacy / FOCAC institutional context.

A future implementation must decide whether existing `TRADE_SANCTIONS_INDUSTRIAL_POLICY` can host a new non-sanctions event type cleanly or whether the taxonomy needs a more general trade-agreement/process event type. **Do not create a new category solely for this programme** without reviewing the current schema and neighboring cases.

## Why CQ should not add the Cabo Verde Live row yet

A bounded Live row could factually record the 9 September signing. However, doing so immediately would discard the more important architectural signal: the source says this is the 40th member of a structured programme with multiple later legal/negotiation phases.

Population first would risk:

- inventing an isolated story identity for a programme event;
- later duplicate identities if the family is modelled properly;
- collapsing signing, negotiation conclusion and effectiveness;
- treating the Chinese news release as sufficient for every partner-specific legal state;
- allowing a convenient primary source to drive coverage without source-rights architecture;
- creating pressure to backfill 39 earlier agreements before methodology is stable.

The correct sequence is architecture/source-family research first, then a deliberately small sample if justified.

## CQ conclusion

**The China–Cabo Verde signing is evidence of a genuine, strategically relevant China–Africa trade/development agreement programme and warrants explicit WORLD SIGNALS architecture/source-family research. It does not yet warrant bulk Canonical backfill, automatic monitoring, or even an immediate Cabo Verde Live row.**

The strongest current design hypothesis is a partner-specific staged process under a common programme family, with signing, substantive negotiation conclusion, instrument signature and effectiveness kept distinct. That hypothesis remains provisional until registry identity and partner-text sampling are completed.
