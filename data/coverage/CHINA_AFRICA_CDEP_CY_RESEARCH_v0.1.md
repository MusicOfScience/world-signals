## Executive finding

China's Common Development Economic Partnership programme with African states is a real, repeatable trade/development-policy family with multiple partner-specific stages.

Fresh primary material now reinforces three distinct facts:

1. Cabo Verde signed the **40th** reported China–Africa framework agreement on 9 September 2026.
2. Cabo Verde and China separately confirmed **substantive conclusion of early-harvest negotiations**; that is not equivalent to an early-harvest agreement being signed or effective.
3. Seychelles and China signed an **Early Harvest Arrangement** on 10 September 2026, independently demonstrating progression beyond framework signature within the same programme family.

The correct WORLD SIGNALS response remains source/identity architecture first, not treaty-catalogue population.

## Source hierarchy for later governance

Preferred roles:

1. MOFCOM treaty/agreement text or dedicated FTA service — instrument/legal text where available;
2. MOFCOM News Office / WTO Department — signing and negotiation milestone;
3. partner government's trade ministry / treaty office / official gazette — counterpart and partner-specific legal effect;
4. PRC MFA / embassy — diplomatic context;
5. FOCAC programme documents — programme context rather than bilateral legal-state authority.

One source identity should not pretend all these roles are interchangeable.

## Rights / automation boundary

Public accessibility and attribution language do not by themselves establish permission for unattended production monitoring or bulk treaty mirroring.

CY therefore recommends that a future MOFCOM source identity, if registered, default to curated/manual provenance use unless a separate endpoint/rights review expressly clears more.

- unattended monitoring permission;
