## Executive finding

The 9 September 2026 China–Cabo Verde Common Development Economic Partnership Framework Agreement is not an isolated bilateral press-release event. MOFCOM evidence establishes a multi-country China–Africa programme with at least 40 framework signings and a real staged implementation grammar that can progress through framework signature, module negotiations, early-harvest arrangements and legal effectiveness.

However, the deeper WORLD SIGNALS identity probe changes the design conclusion from the initial v0.1 hypothesis:

1. **Do not create a 40-row Canonical backfill or a synthetic global Canonical series.** The programme is a useful research/source-family concept, but individual bilateral milestones should enter Canonical only when a specific occurrence is independently worth canonicalising under normal rules.
2. The existing taxonomy already provides a suitable generic event grammar: `TRADE_SANCTIONS_INDUSTRIAL_POLICY / TRADE_POLICY_PROCESS`. A new category is unnecessary, and a new generic event type is not currently justified.
3. No existing Canonical occurrence or Source Registry identity was found for Cabo Verde/FOCAC/CDEP after direct interrogation of the actual post-CP governed files, not merely code-search labels.
4. A later Cabo Verde production step is therefore more naturally a **bounded unlinked Live trade-policy observation first**, unless a separate Canonical reason exists. WORLD SIGNALS must not fabricate a Canonical anchor just to obtain an `OUTCOME_OF` relationship.
5. A new MOFCOM trade source identity will probably be needed for governed population, but **CQ does not allocate one**. Source identity must be designed after a focused source-governance review.
6. Automated monitoring remains closed. MOFCOM reuse language is not unattended-retrieval permission.

## Trigger development — Cabo Verde, 9 September 2026

MOFCOM states that on 9 September 2026 China International Trade Representative and Vice Minister Li Chenggang and Cabo Verde Minister of Economy, Trade, Industry and Digital Transformation António Baptista signed the `Framework Agreement between the Government of the People's Republic of China and the Government of the Republic of Cabo Verde on a Common Development Economic Partnership` in Beijing.

MOFCOM describes Cabo Verde as the **40th African country** with such a framework. The same release separately says the two sides confirmed the **substantive conclusion of negotiations on an early-harvest arrangement**.

Primary source:

- MOFCOM News Office, 10 September 2026: `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2026/art_4b477c9439de4b17a79d39a63456a7d0.html`

The two propositions must remain separate:

- supported: framework agreement **signed**;
- supported: early-harvest negotiations **substantively concluded**;
- not established by this release: early-harvest arrangement signed;
- not established by this release: early-harvest arrangement effective;
- not yet established from partner-specific treaty text in CQ: exact Cabo Verde framework entry-into-force rule.

A current search of Cabo Verde government material did not expose a 9 September counterpart publication for this exact framework. That is a retrieval gap, not negative evidence.

## Canonical vs Live architecture

CQ's current recommendation is **not** to create a programme-wide Canonical family simply because 40 agreements exist.

Reasons:

1. The Canonical Registry is an event identity system and Calendar source, not a treaty encyclopaedia.
2. Many framework signings were already historical before WORLD SIGNALS discovered the programme; bulk backfill would create population pressure without a demonstrated calendar/analysis need.
3. A bilateral CDEP process can have heterogeneous later milestones whose exact timing and legal states are partner-specific.
4. Live Intelligence already supports important unscheduled policy developments without synthetic Canonical anchors.
5. Creating a Canonical occurrence merely so a Live observation can have `OUTCOME_OF` would reverse the architecture.

Recommended rule:

> CDEP programme/family knowledge may guide identity discovery and source selection, but Canonical occurrences should be created only for independently justified bilateral milestones that meet normal Canonical inclusion criteria. Current unscheduled signings may remain unlinked Live observations unless a stable pre-existing Canonical occurrence exists for a real reason.

For Cabo Verde specifically, a later bounded Live observation is plausible. It should not be linked to a newly invented Canonical row solely for graph completeness.

## Rights / automated retrieval boundary

- `https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/fzlm/wzsm/index.html`

states that original material on the ministry site/subsites is copyrighted by the MOFCOM site and that reuse/republication should attribute the source. It distinguishes original from republished/compiled/excerpted material.

CQ finds this sufficient to support careful **curated factual research with attribution** as a working source-governance hypothesis. It does **not** expressly clear unattended automated retrieval, bulk mirroring or a monitoring cadence.

Therefore:

- curated provenance use: candidate for later registry review;
- automated retrieval: `NOT CLEARED`;

- Monitor route: `NOT AUTHORISED`;

## Final CQ research conclusion

The China–Africa CDEP programme is a real and strategically relevant trade/development-policy family, but the correct WORLD SIGNALS response is **not to turn the programme count into a Canonical dataset**.

The strongest current architecture is:

`programme knowledge / source family` → `partner-specific factual milestone` → `Live by default for unscheduled current developments` → `Canonical only when independently justified`.

Existing `TRADE_POLICY_PROCESS` grammar is sufficient at generic event-type level. Source governance, partner-specific legal-state evidence and bounded sample design remain the preconditions for any later production step.

All automatic Canonical, Calendar, Monitor→Live, Live→Analysis and public-projection gates remain closed. OPEC CE quarantine remains untouched.
