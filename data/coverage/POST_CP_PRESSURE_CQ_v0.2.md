## CQ selection after direct registry/source interrogation

Fresh research selects the **China–Africa Common Development Economic Partnership programme** as the best next architecture question, triggered by the 9 September 2026 China–Cabo Verde framework signing.

MOFCOM states that Cabo Verde is the 40th African partner with such a framework and that the two sides also substantively concluded early-harvest negotiations. Historical MOFCOM material proves a multi-year programme and later legal/negotiation phases in other partner cases.

The direct governed-file probe then materially refined the proposed architecture:

1. post-CP Canonical has no `Cabo Verde`, `FOCAC` or `economic partnership` match;
2. post-CP Source Registry has no MOFCOM/PRC Ministry of Commerce source identity;
3. existing trade sources occupy the `WSSRC-TRD-*` family but no future source ID is reserved in CQ;
4. existing Canonical taxonomy **already has** `TRADE_SANCTIONS_INDUSTRIAL_POLICY / TRADE_POLICY_PROCESS`, including the USMCA negotiating-round case;
5. therefore no new category and no new generic event type is justified for CDEP at this point;
6. a programme-wide Canonical backfill is not justified merely because MOFCOM reports 40 partner frameworks.

## Correct architecture pressure

The useful signal is not `Europe is filled, so fill another region`, nor `trade category exists, so add another row`.

The CDEP programme matters because it exposes a repeatable cross-border policy process with distinct legal/negotiation states and a strong Global South institutional context. But the Canonical Registry should not become a treaty catalogue.

CQ therefore recommends this design boundary:

- retain programme/family knowledge in research/source architecture;
- treat unscheduled current partner milestones as Live by default when independently important;
- create Canonical occurrences only when the particular milestone has a separate Canonical justification — scheduled/future tracking, recurring process identity, downstream Analysis requirement, or another Charter-consistent reason;
- never create a Canonical anchor solely to manufacture an `OUTCOME_OF` Live link.

For Cabo Verde, a later single unlinked Live observation is more natural than a synthetic Canonical occurrence, subject to fresh source-governance and evidence preflight.
